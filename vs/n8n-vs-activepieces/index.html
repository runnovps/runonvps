Verdict

The short version

  • The RAM rows are not like-for-like: Activepieces publishes an official 4 GB minimum, while n8n publishes no hardware minimum at all — so n8n’s lower figures are RunOnVPS estimates, not project figures.
  • Our recommended rows put n8n on the 4 GB tier and Activepieces on the 8 GB tier.
  • Licence is the sharpest difference: n8n is fair-code under the Sustainable Use License; Activepieces’ core is MIT, with its ee/ portions under a separate enterprise licence.
  • n8n is far larger on GitHub in our dataset (~208k vs ~25k stars, checked 2026-10-24).

Neither verdict nor table scores the apps against each other — the right pick depends on the job, the licence you can accept, and how much of the spec is published rather than estimated. If you plan to run either alongside other apps, add it to a multi-app VPS estimate →
