Verdict

The short version

  • Recommended RAM is identical in our dataset (4 GB, estimated for both), so the published differences are disk (10 GB vs 30 GB minimum) and CPU disclosure (Coolify publishes 2 cores; Dokploy publishes none).
  • Both are Apache-2.0 at the core; Dokploy’s /proprietary directories are under a separate source-available licence.
  • Coolify is larger on GitHub in our dataset (~64k vs ~38k stars, checked 2026-10-24).
  • Both figures describe the platform itself — whatever you deploy on top still needs its own RAM and disk on the same server.

Neither verdict nor table scores the apps against each other — the right pick depends on the job, the licence you can accept, and how much of the spec is published rather than estimated. If you plan to run either alongside other apps, add it to a multi-app VPS estimate →
