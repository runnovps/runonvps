Why two badges?

Official is not the same as estimated

Many excellent projects never publish a complete hardware minimum. Rather than copying an unsourced number from a hosting blog, RunOnVPS marks those entries as Estimated and explains the documented deployment used for the estimate. A value is only marked Official when the checked source supplied it without an estimate marker in our dataset.
