FAQ

Dify VPS questions

Answers use only the checked dataset on this page — estimates are called out as estimates.

What are the minimum VPS requirements for Dify?

In the RunOnVPS dataset, Dify needs at least RAM 4 GiB, CPU 2 cores. Our suggested starting tier, based on the recommended RAM, is the 8 GB RAM tier.

Does Dify need a GPU?

No. Dify does not need a GPU — the baseline deployment runs on CPU only.

What SaaS product does Dify replace?

Dify is catalogued here as a self-hosted alternative to Dify Cloud. Self-hosting replaces the hosted subscription with your own VPS; feature parity varies, so compare your required workflow before migrating.
