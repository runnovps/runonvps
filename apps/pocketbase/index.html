FAQ

PocketBase VPS questions

Answers use only the checked dataset on this page — estimates are called out as estimates.

What are the minimum VPS requirements for PocketBase?

In the RunOnVPS dataset, PocketBase needs at least RAM 512 MB, CPU 1 vCPU, disk 1 GB. Some of these figures are estimated rather than officially published — the badge next to each value in the table above shows its basis. Our suggested starting tier, based on the recommended RAM, is the 1 GB RAM tier.

Does PocketBase need a GPU?

No. PocketBase does not need a GPU — the baseline deployment runs on CPU only.

What SaaS product does PocketBase replace?

PocketBase is catalogued here as a self-hosted alternative to Supabase / Firebase. Self-hosting replaces the hosted subscription with your own VPS; feature parity varies, so compare your required workflow before migrating.
