FAQ

Dokploy VPS questions

Answers use only the checked dataset on this page — estimates are called out as estimates.

What are the minimum VPS requirements for Dokploy?

In the RunOnVPS dataset, Dokploy needs at least RAM 2 GB, disk 30 GB. Our suggested starting tier, based on the recommended RAM, is the 4 GB RAM tier.

Does Dokploy need a GPU?

No. Dokploy does not need a GPU — the baseline deployment runs on CPU only.

What SaaS product does Dokploy replace?

Dokploy is catalogued here as a self-hosted alternative to Vercel / Netlify / Heroku. Self-hosting replaces the hosted subscription with your own VPS; feature parity varies, so compare your required workflow before migrating.
