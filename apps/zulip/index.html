FAQ

Zulip VPS questions

Answers use only the checked dataset on this page — estimates are called out as estimates.

What are the minimum VPS requirements for Zulip?

In the RunOnVPS dataset, Zulip needs at least RAM 2 GB, CPU 1 vCPU, disk 10 GB. Our suggested starting tier, based on the recommended RAM, is the 4 GB RAM tier.

Does Zulip need a GPU?

No. Zulip does not need a GPU — the baseline deployment runs on CPU only.

What SaaS product does Zulip replace?

Zulip is catalogued here as a self-hosted alternative to Slack / Zulip Cloud. Self-hosting replaces the hosted subscription with your own VPS; feature parity varies, so compare your required workflow before migrating.

Source check

Important notes

Official requirements docs (checked 2026-11-16): at least 2 GB RAM (2 GB swap recommended under 5 GB), 1 CPU sufficient under 100 users; 100+ users need 2 CPUs and 4 GB RAM; 10 GB free disk space (approx 25 GB total disk with OS). Installer expects Zulip to be the only thing on the system; supported OS Ubuntu 22.04/24.04/26.04 and Debian 12/13. Official Docker image also available (zulip/docker-zulip) but needs secrets files plus compose.override.yaml, so the installer route is recommended and no trimmed compose snippet is given.
