FAQ

Penpot VPS questions

Answers use only the checked dataset on this page — estimates are called out as estimates.

What are the minimum VPS requirements for Penpot?

In the RunOnVPS dataset, Penpot needs at least RAM 4 GB, CPU 2 vCPU, disk 10 GB. Some of these figures are estimated rather than officially published — the badge next to each value in the table above shows its basis. Our suggested starting tier, based on the recommended RAM, is the 8 GB RAM tier.

Does Penpot need a GPU?

No. Penpot does not need a GPU — the baseline deployment runs on CPU only.

What SaaS product does Penpot replace?

Penpot is catalogued here as a self-hosted alternative to Figma. Self-hosting replaces the hosted subscription with your own VPS; feature parity varies, so compare your required workflow before migrating.

Source check

Important notes

Official docs (checked 2026-11-03) publish no single minimum: the official help page says 4 CPUs and 16 GB RAM are sufficient to support thousands of users and gives a 50-100 GB disk rule of thumb for up to 10 editors, while the official Penpot blog Docker guide plans at least 1-2 CPUs, 4 GiB RAM and 10 GB disk; all sizing values here are conservative estimates for a small-team deployment from the official multi-service Docker Compose (frontend, backend, exporter, PostgreSQL, Valkey) in penpot/penpot docker/images, which requires PENPOT_PUBLIC_URI and a generated PENPOT_SECRET_KEY plus a reverse proxy for HTTPS.
