FAQ

Outline VPS questions

Answers use only the checked dataset on this page — estimates are called out as estimates.

What are the minimum VPS requirements for Outline?

In the RunOnVPS dataset, Outline needs at least RAM 2 GB, CPU 2 vCPU, disk 10 GB. Some of these figures are estimated rather than officially published — the badge next to each value in the table above shows its basis. Our suggested starting tier, based on the recommended RAM, is the 4 GB RAM tier.

Does Outline need a GPU?

No. Outline does not need a GPU — the baseline deployment runs on CPU only.

What SaaS product does Outline replace?

Outline is catalogued here as a self-hosted alternative to Notion / Confluence. Self-hosting replaces the hosted subscription with your own VPS; feature parity varies, so compare your required workflow before migrating.
