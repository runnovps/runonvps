FAQ

Audiobookshelf VPS questions

Answers use only the checked dataset on this page — estimates are called out as estimates.

What are the minimum VPS requirements for Audiobookshelf?

In the RunOnVPS dataset, Audiobookshelf needs at least RAM 1GB, CPU 1, disk 5GB. Some of these figures are estimated rather than officially published — the badge next to each value in the table above shows its basis. Our suggested starting tier, based on the recommended RAM, is the 2 GB RAM tier.

Does Audiobookshelf need a GPU?

No. Audiobookshelf does not need a GPU — the baseline deployment runs on CPU only.

What SaaS product does Audiobookshelf replace?

Audiobookshelf is catalogued here as a self-hosted alternative to Audible. Self-hosting replaces the hosted subscription with your own VPS; feature parity varies, so compare your required workflow before migrating.
