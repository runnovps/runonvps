FAQ

Discourse VPS questions

Answers use only the checked dataset on this page — estimates are called out as estimates.

What are the minimum VPS requirements for Discourse?

In the RunOnVPS dataset, Discourse needs at least RAM 1 GB, CPU 1 vCPU, disk 10 GB. Our suggested starting tier, based on the recommended RAM, is the 2 GB RAM tier.

Does Discourse need a GPU?

No. Discourse does not need a GPU — the baseline deployment runs on CPU only.

What SaaS product does Discourse replace?

Discourse is catalogued here as a self-hosted alternative to Discourse hosting / Vanilla Forums. Self-hosting replaces the hosted subscription with your own VPS; feature parity varies, so compare your required workflow before migrating.

Important notes

Official cloud install guide (checked 2026-11-03): 1 GB RAM minimum with 2 GB swap, 2 GB or more recommended; 1 core minimum, 2 or more preferred; 10 GB disk minimum, 20 GB recommended. Install is the supported Docker path only: clone discourse_docker, run ./discourse-setup (creates swap when RAM is 2 GB or less), and a domain plus working SMTP are required.
